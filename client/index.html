Pryvo — Find Someone Who Gets You

Pryvo is a modern 18+ dating app developed by Traincape Technologies Pvt Ltd in New Delhi, India. Built for genuine intentions and respectful conversations, Pryvo lets adults discover compatible people nearby, express their personality through prompts and photos, see who likes them, and chat in real time — 100% free with zero hidden paywalls in v1.

About Pryvo & How It Works

Create an expressive profile with photos, conversation prompts, and interests. Use distance and match preferences to discover nearby adults without exposing your exact GPS location. Match mutually and start real-time conversations in a calm, distraction-free chat interface.

  • Expressive prompt-driven profiles designed for meaningful conversations
  • See who likes you and match freely with no hidden subscriptions
  • Instant real-time chat with typing indicators and safety controls
  • Available on Android (package com.pryvo)

Privacy, Safety & Content Moderation

Pryvo is strictly for adults aged 18 and older. Uploaded profile photos are screened using automated AI content moderation, profile text and comments are filtered for abuse, and every profile and chat includes one-tap block and report controls. We enforce a zero-tolerance Child Safety (CSAE) policy and provide immediate self-service account deletion in-app and online.
