# Pryvo — Find Someone Who Gets You

**Pryvo** is a modern 18+ dating application built by **Traincape Technologies Pvt Ltd** (New Delhi, India) for adults looking for genuine, respectful connections.

- **Platform:** Android (`com.pryvo`)
- **Pricing (v1):** 100% Free — discovery, seeing who likes you, matching, and real-time chat with no hidden paywalls
- **Eligibility:** Adults 18+ only
- **Support & Privacy Email:** [pryvo@traincapetech.in](mailto:pryvo@traincapetech.in)

## About Pryvo

Pryvo prioritizes meaningful conversations over mindless swiping. Every profile highlights real photos, expressive prompts, interests, and location-based discovery preferences so adults can connect over shared values.

## Key Features

- **Expressive Profiles:** Share photos, custom prompts, lifestyle preferences, and interests.
- **Distance & Preference Filters:** Discover compatible adults nearby while keeping exact GPS coordinates private.
- **See Who Likes You:** Review incoming likes and match freely without paywalls.
- **Real-Time Safe Chat:** Instant messaging with read receipts, typing indicators, and instant safety actions.
- **Safety & Moderation:** Automated AI photo moderation (Sightengine), server-side text screening, instant blocking, one-tap reporting, and zero-tolerance Child Safety (CSAE) enforcement.
- **Full Data Control:** Delete your account and personal data anytime in-app or online via email OTP verification at [/delete-account](https://pryvo.traincapetech.in/delete-account).
